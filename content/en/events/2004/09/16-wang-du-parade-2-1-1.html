Fourth stop of the Wang Du Parade at the Palais de Tokyo in Paris

body: >-

Introduction

At the Palais de Tokyo, the fourth stop on the “Wang Du Parade” invites viewers to immerse themselves in a journey that is as playful as it is spectacular, at the heart of Wang Du’s artistic universe. The exhibition is presented as a series of sculptural spaces that visitors physically traverse, moving from one media-based environment to another. This immersive experience brings together five works: Tunnel d'espace-temps and oaribbeancom , created specifically for the exhibition, as well as Tapis volant , World Markets, and Défilé .
