Descriptive and tactile visit summary: >-

On the occasion of the exhibition Bienvenue à La Criée! persons with visual impairments, accompanying persons and friends are invited to participate in sensory mediation around exhibited works. Specializing in taste visits, Capucine Priser proposes to reactivate sensory memory of the place during a time of exchange, conviviality and sharing. The visit of the exhibition will be enhanced by tasting small birthday cakes with local and seasonal fruits and vegetables, echoing the history of the place.
