For the past 40 years, La Criée centre d'art contemporain and le Fonds régional d'art contemporain (Frac) Bretagne have collaborated on a very regular basis on the production of artworks, exhibitions, outreach projects, publications, and study days. As part of Immersion, la collection du Frac Bretagne du sol au plafond, a special feature highlights these fruitful exchanges between the Frac and La Criée by presenting a selection of works acquired in connection with an exhibition at the art center. The selection focuses on the late 1980s and early 1990s, featuring works by François Bouillon, Patrice Carré, Gilles Mahé, Maria Nordman, and Yvan Salomone.
