Gilles Amalvi Concert summary: >-

Gilles Amalvi, a sound artist and poet, has been presenting a sound piece at the art center since the opening of the “Bienvenue à La Criée” exhibition. It is a collection of testimonials featuring the words of artists and visitors recalling memories of exhibitions, residencies, or encounters at La Criée.
The art center invites you to experience a live version of this archival work.
