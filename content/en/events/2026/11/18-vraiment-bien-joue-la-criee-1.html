Excerpt from “Rejouer La Criée” in LSF (French Sign Language) summary: >-

“Rejouer La Criée,” an art object and interactive educational tool created to celebrate La Criée’s 40th anniversary, is presented by Zoé Crescoli in a segment featuring French Sign Language (LSF).
This is an opportunity for everyone to discover the history of the art center and the many exhibitions it has produced over the past 40 years through a board game designed by the artist collective Léa Bénétou, Fanny Martel, and Yann Baïzid, in close collaboration with the art center’s team!
