La Criée is inviting musician and visual artist Grégory Hairon for a research, creation and knowledge-sharing residency at Jacques Prévert Primary School in Rennes. His project involves engaging pupils from Years 1 to 3 (ages 6 to 8) in the creation of sound sculptures, in dialogue with La Criée's artistic programme, « Laisser la vie déborder »

body: >-

About

For his project at Jacques Prévert Primary School, artist Grégory Hairon, aka Gregaldur or Héron Cendré, is developing the idea of creating random poetry machines in collaboration with pupils from Year 1 to Year 3 (ages 6 to 8). These will take the form of sound sculptures made from reclaimed materials, school desks, small motors and electronic systems. The artist and pupils will experiment with three-dimensional art-making and sound creation, in the spirit of outsider art. The workshops will also be open to participation by after-school groups and will culminate in an installation featuring a scenography and sculptures that visitors can activate. Throughout the project, the pupils will take part in discussions and listening sessions exploring Grégory Hairon's artistic practice, as well as visits to La Criée.

Project schedule
Grégory Hairon will be in residence at the school during the following periods: -- 7-11 December 2026 -- 11-29 January 2027 -- 15 March-2 April 2027 The artist will lead workshops with the classes in the mornings. Afternoons will be devoted to artistic research in the residency studio, located on the first floor of the after-school centre building. Key dates -- Thursday 28 January, 4:30 pm: Concert and opening of the studio to families and partners of the Bréquigny Cité éducative. -- Thursday 8 April, 4:30 pm: Exhibition opening at the school.
