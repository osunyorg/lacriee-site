The exhibition presents the works produced by the support classes of CE1 by Camille Djedi, Vincent Krier, Noémie Pailler and CP's satellite classes CE1 by Sabrina Paillard, Marie Pannetier and Anne Broussot, with the artists Jérémy Piningre and Aëla Mai Cabel. It is produced within the framework of the residence Les appareils et les Ktronics conducted with La Criée art centre contemporary.

body: >-

About

From January to April 2026, artist, author and illustrator Jérémy Piningre worked with pupils to create new worlds inspired by his illustrated book Les Fruits et les Gumes , published in 2023. Drawing on familiar places in the Bréquigny neighbourhood, the children imagined settings, invented characters and brought a multitude of little stories to life. The exhibition presents the different stages of this collective adventure. Drawings, cut-out characters, stick puppets and large colourful backdrops come together to form a series of worlds waiting to be explored. A giant leporello - an accordion-fold book displayed across the exhibition space - brings together drawings from a cherche et trouve (search-and-find) game devised by the pupils. Characters created by the different classes also appear on large-scale panels, giving a new dimension to these imaginary figures. The residency also offered an opportunity to discover the behind-the-scenes process of making a book. With the help of Aëla Maï Cabel, who co-founded the Cardon Luneux publishing house with Jérémy Piningre, the pupils took part in every stage of creating a fanzine: organising the pages, choosing titles, designing the layout, and making the cover and endpapers. Workshops at édulab-Pasteur introduced them to risograph printing, a technique used to produce this collective publication, printed in a limited edition. Through this exhibition, the pupils share the worlds they have imagined, the characters they have invented and the stories they have enjoyed telling. Visitors are invited to step into these universes and, in turn, let their own imaginations run free.
