Exhibition of CP-CE1 students' creations, with artists Jérémy Piningre and Aëla Mai Cabel, followed by a spring snack.

body: >-

About

The exhibition presents works created by the CE1 classes led by Camille Djedi, Vincent Krier and Noémie Pailler, and the CP-CE1 satellite classes led by Sabrina Paillard, Marie Pannetier and Anne Broussot, in collaboration with artists Jérémy Piningre and Aëla Maï Cabel. It is part of Les appareils et les Ktronics , an artist residency organised in partnership with La Criée centre d'art contemporain. From January to April 2026, artist, author and illustrator Jérémy Piningre invited the pupils to imagine possible continuations of his illustrated book Les Fruits et les Gumes , published in 2023. The project focused on storytelling, the creation of "mini-worlds" and the characters who inhabit them.The residency unfolded in three stages. First, the pupils developed settings for their future stories using photographs taken in the Bréquigny neighbourhood. Their imagination turned to familiar everyday places such as the hair salon, the park and its playground equipment, the grocery shop, and so on. Once these spaces had been reinterpreted through drawing, the children invented characters to populate these new territories. They gave them names, then imagined their habits, personalities, ways of speaking and relationships with other characters. In the second stage, the pupils brought their characters to life as stick puppets, set against large, colourful backdrops painted in the classroom. They then came up with stories based on the scenes they had created.The pupils also designed cherche et trouve (search-and-find) games inspired by the opening pages of Les Fruits et les Gumes . The aim is to find the drawing on one page that corresponds to the word written on another. These drawings are presented here as a giant leporello, an accordion-fold book that unfolds throughout the exhibition space. The pupils from the satellite classes were responsible for creating the invitation cards, conceived as a large set of collectible playing cards. Inspired by the characters invented by their classmates in the main classes, they drew their own characters and environments on coloured paper. They then cut out these elements and rearranged them on a large white sheet, following the visual approach of Les Fruits et les Gumes , in which everything depicted is conceived as a collection of small objects placed on top of one another. Some of these characters are displayed here in enlarged form, mounted on custom-cut PVC panels. On speech bubbles of various sizes, the pupils have written words of welcome in languages of their choice (French, languages spoken at home, invented languages, and more), using alphabets hand-drawn by pupils from the main classes and by the artists.The final stage of the residency was devoted to creating a fanzine, a handmade publication, in collaboration with Aëla Maï Cabel, who co-founded the Cardon Luneux publishing house with Jérémy Piningre. The three main classes took part in every stage of the book's production: organising the chapters and page sequence, designing the layout, writing the titles, and creating the cover and endpapers. The pupils also discovered risograph printing during a series of printing workshops at édulab-Pasteur. The exhibition presents this collaborative fanzine, published in a limited edition. The pupils will in turn have the opportunity to discover works by Jérémy Piningre and Aëla Maï Cabel at La Criée from 12 June onwards!

The exhibition presents works created by the CE1 classes led by Camille Djedi, Vincent Krier and Noémie Pailler, and the CP-CE1 satellite classes led by Sabrina Paillard, Marie Pannetier and Anne Broussot, in collaboration with artists Jérémy Piningre and Aëla Maï Cabel. It is part of Les appareils et les Ktronics, an artist residency organised in partnership with La Criée centre d'art contemporain.

From January to April 2026, artist, author and illustrator Jérémy Piningre invited the pupils to imagine possible continuations of his illustrated book Les Fruits et les Gumes, published in 2023. The project focused on storytelling, the creation of "mini-worlds" and the characters who inhabit them.The residency unfolded in three stages. First, the pupils developed settings for their future stories using photographs taken in the Bréquigny neighbourhood. Their imagination turned to familiar everyday places such as the hair salon, the park and its playground equipment, the grocery shop, and so on. Once these spaces had been reinterpreted through drawing, the children invented characters to populate these new territories. They gave them names, then imagined their habits, personalities, ways of speaking and relationships with other characters.

In the second stage, the pupils brought their characters to life as stick puppets, set against large, colourful backdrops painted in the classroom. They then came up with stories based on the scenes they had created.The pupils also designed cherche et trouve (search-and-find) games inspired by the opening pages of Les Fruits et les Gumes. The aim is to find the drawing on one page that corresponds to the word written on another. These drawings are presented here as a giant leporello, an accordion-fold book that unfolds throughout the exhibition space.

The pupils from the satellite classes were responsible for creating the invitation cards, conceived as a large set of collectible playing cards. Inspired by the characters invented by their classmates in the main classes, they drew their own characters and environments on coloured paper. They then cut out these elements and rearranged them on a large white sheet, following the visual approach of Les Fruits et les Gumes, in which everything depicted is conceived as a collection of small objects placed on top of one another.

Some of these characters are displayed here in enlarged form, mounted on custom-cut PVC panels. On speech bubbles of various sizes, the pupils have written words of welcome in languages of their choice (French, languages spoken at home, invented languages, and more), using alphabets hand-drawn by pupils from the main classes and by the artists.The final stage of the residency was devoted to creating a fanzine, a handmade publication, in collaboration with Aëla Maï Cabel, who co-founded the Cardon Luneux publishing house with Jérémy Piningre. The three main classes took part in every stage of the book's production: organising the chapters and page sequence, designing the layout, writing the titles, and creating the cover and endpapers. The pupils also discovered risograph printing during a series of printing workshops at édulab-Pasteur. The exhibition presents this collaborative fanzine, published in a limited edition.

The pupils will in turn have the opportunity to discover works by Jérémy Piningre and Aëla Maï Cabel at La Criée from 12 June onwards!
