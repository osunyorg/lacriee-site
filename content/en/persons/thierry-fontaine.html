Thierry Fontaine (born 1969 in Saint-Pierre, Réunion) is a French artist and photographer who lives and works in Paris.
Graduated from the Ecole Supérieure des Arts Décoratifs in Strasbourg in 1992. In 1999, he was a resident at the Villa Médicis in Rome.
