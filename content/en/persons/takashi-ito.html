Takashi Itō (伊 藤 孝 之), born in 1894 near Hamamatsu, Shizuoka Prefecture and died 9 May 1982 was a painter and draftsman of Japanese prints from the shin-hanga movement, known for his depictions of landscapes (Fukei-ga).
