Laurent Duthion, born in Rennes (France) on April 7, 1972, is a French visual artist and writer. His plastic practice is particularly extensive since it addresses fields as diverse as medical or horticultural research, molecular chemistry, experimental music, science fiction, and cooking. This diversity which intervenes as much in the theoretical field of his work as in the conception and the realization of his works brings him regularly to collaborate with scientists. In 2007, he went to Antarctica with researchers and participated in the Ichtyology coastal missions in Adélie Land (ICOTA) and the ALBION2 campaign. In 2010, he published his first book Transsubstantiation etc.
