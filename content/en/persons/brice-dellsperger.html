Brice Dellsperger is a French artist born in 1972, who lives in Paris.

Since 1995, he has been working on remakes of cult movie sequences (Pulsions, Return of the Jedi, Saturday Night Fever, The Important Thing is to Love, My Own Private Idaho, Twin Peaks...). He directed about thirty of them, which he collected under the generic title of Body Double.

He teaches at the École nationale supérieure des arts décoratifs, in Paris.
