Born in La Hague and based in Rennes for the past ten years, Grégory Hairon (aka Gregaldur or Héron Cendré) is a multidisciplinary artist whose distinctive universe lies at the crossroads of minimalist music, sound experimentation, live performance and three-dimensional visual arts. A prolific artist, he composes electronic music under the name Héron Cendré and designs musical instruments and interactive sound installations, combining electronic devices, field recordings and recycled materials. Under his other stage name, Gregaldur, he develops projects combining live cinema, performance, object theatre and puppet-based installations. He also works as an actor in theatre and film. Across all his projects, he champions a playful, inventive approach that is accessible to all audiences, where scenography and music meet the world of outsider art.
