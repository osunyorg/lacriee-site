8 mm film, black-and-white, silent, 3 min








The film Des oliviers was shot in the summer of 2013 at an olive grove in the hills above Saïda. The black-and-white images evoke the region’s history and economy, as well as the ancestral farming practices that continue to this day. The film features close-ups of olives, branches, machinery, the sky, the hands of olive pickers, and more. Ziad Antar edited this film “like a poem, a metaphor.”








courtesy Almine Rech Gallery, Paris / Bruxelles
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
