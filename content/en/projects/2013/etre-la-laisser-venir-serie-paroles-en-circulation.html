wall painting, acrylic, roller, 1000 x 300 cm / 1600 x 300 cm











A mural featuring a quote collected by the Ripoulain Brothers during their community service is created using a typography constrained by a roller on the large, dilapidated wall of the Guillaume Régnier Hospital. This phrase, “être là laisser venir” (“be there, let it come”), aims to evoke both the specific temporality of interaction within the Guillaume Régnier Hospital and that of observing urban margins—heterotopias—as spaces that serve as resources for various practices.











Soutiens : -- ville de Rennes -- centre hospitalier Guillaume Régnier, Rennes -- ministère de la Culture / Drac Bretagne -- ministère de la Santé / ARS Bretagne
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
