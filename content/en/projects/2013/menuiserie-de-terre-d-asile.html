Les Frères Ripoulain

body: >-


treillage, découpe de bois, peinture acrylique, vernis, dimensions variables

















Menuiserie de terre d’asile  is a chaotic latticework structure onto which are grafted silhouettes shaped like recognizable architectural elements, plants, furniture, and other remarkable objects collected during the Ripoulain brothers’ compulsory labor. Menuiserie de terre d’asile  refers to garden carpentry and shifts this ornamental practice toward an analytical examination of the visual environmental elements that give this place its uniqueness. Here, “asylum” is to be understood as a heterotopia—an inviolable place where one is sheltered from persecution, prosecution, and the law.
Menuiserie de terre d’asile  Installed in situ, the work draws on the technique of collage and invites viewers to engage in a sort of visual game in which they search for each silhouette shape hanging on the trellis to match it with its real-world counterpart.
















Supports : -- ville de Rennes -- centre hospitalier Guillaume Régnier, Rennes -- ministère de la Culture / Drac Bretagne -- ministère de la Santé / ARS Bretagne
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
