video, color, sound, 40 seconds










The Night of Love series (Vol. 1, 2009, and Vol. 2, 2013) features different interpretations of one of Oum Kalthoum’s songs, “Laylit Hobb.” This popular song, “rooted in the collective memory of the Arab world,” is partially reinterpreted by two musicians with very different styles and backgrounds: Hsin Jung on violin and Charbel Haber on guitar. In this second part—a new work produced for the exhibition—Charbel Haber performs the song as a tribute to Omar Khorchid, the renowned Egyptian guitarist who played alongside the singer. Through still shots of the musicians, filmed with DV and HD cameras, the paradox and richness of the music—which is both deeply rooted in culture and utterly universal—come into focus.










courtesy Almine Rech Gallery, Paris / Bruxelles
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
