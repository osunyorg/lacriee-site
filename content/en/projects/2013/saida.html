body: >-


8 mm film, color, silent, 3 min










In the main hall, Ziad Antar presents a previously unseen work titled Saïda (2013). Shot with an 8-mm camera from a fishing boat, the film offers a panoramic view of the city, from north to south. The color images, with their blue hues, reveal the harbor, the city’s hills, its transformations, and finally, the landfill that empties into the sea—the largest open-air landfill in the Mediterranean. The film stock and the light give the landscape a distinctive texture. The film interweaves moving exterior shots with interior shots focused on the fish stalls at the market.









courtesy Almine Rech Gallery, Paris / Bruxelles
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
