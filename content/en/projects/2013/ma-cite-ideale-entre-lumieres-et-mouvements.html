Installation, Scale 1 wooden slats, cardboard, paper, recycled materials audio and video equipment lights 

















For his residency at the Jean Moulin School, Lucas Grandin invited students from Rennes and Douala to envision their ideal city through light and movement. At Jean Moulin School in Rennes, Lucas Grandin worked with kindergarten students to create an architectural vision at the intersection of dreams and childhood, through a giant installation combining lights, recycled objects, low-tech elements, shadow puppets, videos, sound machines, and more.

















Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes

Supports : -- ministère de la Culture / Drac Bretagne -- ville de Rennes / DEE -- contrat urbain de cohésion sociale Partners : -- académie de Rennes / DSDEN35 -- école Jean Moulin, Rennes -- école New Bell, Douala, Cameroun
