various materials, 1750 x 770 x 820 cm







The monumental installation Grand Ensemble takes up nearly the entire space of the art center. A rhizomatic work, it consists of hundreds of rod-shaped objects: sticks, branches, broomsticks, curtain rods, bicycle pumps, telescopic antennas, rulers, tubes, and so on. These objects, collected by Jan Kopp from residents of Rennes in the lead-up to the exhibition, are conceived by the artist as “personal ‘standard meters’ moved from various points throughout the city of Rennes and tracing an imaginary geography or network materialized by their temporary assembly in the art center.” The only limits to the expansion of this “Ensemble,” which evolves randomly depending on the results of the collections, are the physical durability of the artwork and the spatial limitations of the art center. A collective work—or at least a collaborative one—this Grand Ensemble reveals some of the artist’s concerns: doing things together and living together. These interconnected objects are like fragments of individual stories that intersect and intertwine, much like a visual variation on Perec’s Life: A User’s Manual.






Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
