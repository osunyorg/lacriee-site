5 photolithographs on black paper, 73 x 55 cm each




Souches  is a series consisting of three black photolithographs on black paper. Playing on reflections and variations in light, these works offer glimpses of foliage and branch patterns as the viewer moves around them.



Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes


Lutz & Guggisberg, vue de l'exposition, La Forêt , La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes, 2013  © Benoît Mauras
