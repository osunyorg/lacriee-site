photographic series, 40 color prints mounted on aluminum 42 x 30 cm each



The series of photographs Hole in the Mirror runs the length of the exhibition space, serving as a common thread that reveals the artists’ particular interest in passageways, mirrors, and reflections. The result of nearly four years of work, this collection brings together several photographs taken around the world: historical artifacts in museums, an aquarium, a Christmas display window, a tunnel through a mountain, a puddle in a gravel pit in Hungary, a bike shed in Meilen, and more. These images are windows onto the world and its civilizations, inviting us on an imaginary journey across the Earth’s surface.


Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes


Lutz & Guggisberg, vue de l'exposition, La Forêt , La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes, 2013  © Benoît Mauras
