wood, various dimensions


Grande Boucle is a suspended wooden sculpture that encircles the light rail at La Criée, serving as a canopy. The various natural and industrial wood elements come together to form an endless Grande Boucle.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
