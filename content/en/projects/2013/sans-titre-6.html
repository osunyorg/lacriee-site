mural 






Sans titre  is a mural that covers the walls of La Criée, some of which are already adorned with abstract landscapes. The splashes of green and black evoke the forest’s world, which is both dark and lush. The murals also highlight the architecture of La Criée, playfully subverting the traditions of fresco painting and abstraction.





Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes


Lutz & Guggisberg, vue de l'exposition, La Forêt , La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes, 2013  © Benoît Mauras
