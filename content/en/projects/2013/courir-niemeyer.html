looping video








Courir Niemeyer is created from video recordings of several artists who participated in the Lebanese leg of the Suspended Spaces project, of which Jan Kopp is an active member. In it, he is seen running on the site of Oscar Niemeyer’s unfinished International Fair in Tripoli. This film is representative of the themes of Jan Kopp’s exhibition and, more broadly, of the work he produced during the Courir les Rues season as an associate artist: vanishing lines and the horizon, the utopian or conflict-ridden city, intersections, exchanges, and transmission.







Production : -- Suspended spaces -- La Criée centre d’art contemporain, Rennes Video recording : -- Cécile Bourgade -- Marcel Dinahet -- Maïder Fortuné -- Nessrine Khodr -- Françoise Parfait -- Éric Valette Editing : Jan Kopp
