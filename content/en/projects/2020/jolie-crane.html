sound piece, 14 min 37 sec


This work was created in April 2020 during the lockdown resulting from the COVID-19 health crisis. It presents, in a format new to both artists, the first phase of the project Where I Am, Where I Come From, Where I’m Going , based on the collections of the Musée de Bretagne and the Musée des Beaux-Arts de Rennes, in collaboration with middle school students from La Binquenais. During a workshop in March 2020, the artists recorded about twenty stories created by two 7th- and 9th-grade classes based on collected objects, with the goal of creating The Collection of Amazing Researchers Aurélie Ferruel and Florentine Guédon chose one of these stories ( La Brauve ) as the starting point for a piece blending various sound elements collected both near and far from where they live.The work reflects the working reality and daily lives of the artists in lockdown and their ability to rethink their creative processes, in line with their collaborative approach to creation. This sound piece is composed of and blends various raw materials: -- the voices of three students from the Collège de la Binquenais, who tell the story of La Brauve; -- telephone recordings of the families living apart from Aurélie Ferruel and Florentine Guédon (who, for this project, became musicians, singers, actors, and contributors to the more intimate exchanges); -- audio recordings of their loved ones who were in quarantine with them (who also became musicians, singers, and actors in the more intimate exchanges); -- recordings of the people with whom they worked during this lockdown (interviews); -- two clips from YouTube tutorials ( Apprendre le rock et Comment faire un beau brushing ) ; -- a nature documentary, Lyre Bird  : National Geographic; -- a song the artists wrote and recorded <3: Jolie Crâne ; -- their phone interviews.

sound piece, 14 min 37 sec

- kind: block template: chapter ranks: base: 2 top: active: false data: layout: no_background text: >-

This work was created in April 2020 during the lockdown resulting from the COVID-19 health crisis. It presents, in a format new to both artists, the first phase of the project Where I Am, Where I Come From, Where I’m Going, based on the collections of the Musée de Bretagne and the Musée des Beaux-Arts de Rennes, in collaboration with middle school students from La Binquenais.

During a workshop in March 2020, the artists recorded about twenty stories created by two 7th- and 9th-grade classes based on collected objects, with the goal of creating The Collection of Amazing Researchers Aurélie Ferruel and Florentine Guédon chose one of these stories (La Brauve) as the starting point for a piece blending various sound elements collected both near and far from where they live.The work reflects the working reality and daily lives of the artists in lockdown and their ability to rethink their creative processes, in line with their collaborative approach to creation.

This sound piece is composed of and blends various raw materials:

-- the voices of three students from the Collège de la Binquenais, who tell the story of La Brauve;

-- telephone recordings of the families living apart from Aurélie Ferruel and Florentine Guédon (who, for this project, became musicians, singers, actors, and contributors to the more intimate exchanges);

-- audio recordings of their loved ones who were in quarantine with them (who also became musicians, singers, and actors in the more intimate exchanges);

-- recordings of the people with whom they worked during this lockdown (interviews);

-- two clips from YouTube tutorials (Apprendre le rock et Comment faire un beau brushing) ;

-- a nature documentary, Lyre Bird : National Geographic;

-- a song the artists wrote and recorded <3: Jolie Crâne;

-- their phone interviews.
