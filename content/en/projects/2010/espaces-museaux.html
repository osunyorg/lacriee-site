20 color photographic prints, 300 g paper, 110 x 77 cm photographs created through the construction of models and digital processing




Alongside her residency, Muriel Bordier produced a series titled Espaces muséaux , consisting of about twenty personal photographs. These photographs were subsequently exhibited at the Carré d’art in Chartres-de-Bretagne, at the Galerie Bailly in Paris, and at the Galerie du Thabor in Rennes.




Supports : -- ministère de la Culture / Drac Bretagne -- ville de Rennes Partners : -- académie de Rennes / DSDEN35 -- école Torigné, Rennes
