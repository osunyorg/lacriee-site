High-gloss photograph mounted on 1.5 mm aluminum, high-gloss lamination, 100 x 100 cm, edition size: 5 + 1 artist’s proof



The English title of this image refers to the Clint Eastwood movie  L’inspecteur ne renonce jamais . The cinematic atmosphere of this work’s subject matter is captured in a strictly photographic format using medium format film. The faded, overexposed colors of the image evoke family photo albums taken with a Kodak Instamatic. This work explores the ability of the collective image to become individualized.


Production Assistance : La Criée centre d’art contemporain, Rennes
