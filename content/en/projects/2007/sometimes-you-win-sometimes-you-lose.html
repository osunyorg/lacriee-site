PVC tubes, blower motor, tube dimensions: 500 cm long x 300 cm maximum height; dimensions may vary depending on the installation in the space


Sometimes you win, sometimes you lose is an American expression that holds particular interest for me because it sums up quite well the spirit of neoliberalism with its “winners” and “losers.” It’s also utterly absurd—the next line being Sometimes it’s raining… An Introduction to the Concept of Chance—Mallarmé’s “A roll of the dice will never abolish chance…”—or the determinism of the contemporary world? I invited about forty artists to contribute to an edition, an artists’ book to this sentence I also spelled out this sentence using plastic pipes stretching 50 meters long and three meters high, then I threw the sentence onto the ground. Amid this chaos of pipes, an object moves at high speed. The viewer attempts to reconstruct the phrase based on the sound path of the object as it travels through the pipe circuit. This futile yet playful attempt serves to neutralize the authoritarian meaning of the phrase and the ideology underlying it. The distorted sentence unfolds across different spaces, compressed or expanded depending on the context. Alexandre Perigot,  Alexandre Perigot : Palaispopeye , Museu Coleção Berardo, Lisbonne ; La Criée centre d’art contemporain, Rennes ; Cneai, Chatou,  2008

Co-production : Tramway, Glasgow
Collection : -- Museu Coleção Berardo, Lisbonne -- courtesy galerie Suzanne Tarasiève, Paris
