High-gloss photograph mounted on 1.5 mm aluminum, high-gloss lamination, 100 x 100 cm, edition size: 5 + 1 artist’s proof


Created during an artist residency in Annecy, this image explores the concepts of the private and public aspects of a work of art within a given territory. This work subverts the strategy employed by social and political structures to situate the artist’s work within the social sphere. It was through an experiment in immersion within these very structures that I deconstructed and displaced the subject—the dress, the figure, the location—to situate it in a world distinct from the everyday life of a neighborhood. This work challenges the usual stereotypes regarding the integration of a population and its participation in the creation of a work of art.

Production Assistance : La Criée centre d’art contemporain, Rennes
