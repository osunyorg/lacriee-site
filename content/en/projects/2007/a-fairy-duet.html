body: >-


2 high-gloss photographs mounted on 1.5 mm aluminum, high-gloss lamination, 100 x 100 cm each, edition size: 5 + 1 artist’s proof




Like the photographic work The Pool of Tears , which belongs to the Frac Bretagne, or The Queens Croquet Ground , which belongs to the Frac Haute-Normandie, the title of this work refers to the book *The Complete Illustrated Works of Lewis Carroll*, published by Chancellor Press in 1982. This title, borrowed from literature, shifts the focus of visual interpretation into the realm of literature while avoiding a mere illustration of a text. But what are these young women doing? Are they reading a book or typing a text message? This diptych attempts to reconstruct a third image in the viewer’s imagination and interpretation.



Production : La Criée centre d’art contemporain, Rennes Collection : fonds départemental d’acquisition d’œuvres d’Ille-et-Vilaine
