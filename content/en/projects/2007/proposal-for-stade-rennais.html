« A Suggestion for Stade Rennais As a defender, you’re taught that in a dangerous situation, you have to clear the ball immediately. Every week there’s a moment during the game when the defender kicks the ball high into the air with a powerful strike, and for just a moment—along with all the other fans in the stadium—I believe it’s going to fly over the stands. How much force would it take to launch a balloon twenty meters into the sky, or sixty meters to carry it out of the stadium? I’d really like to know. There’s a tradition in Edinburgh: every day at 1:00 p.m., a cannon is fired from the castle ramparts over the city. What if we adopted this ceremony for ourselves? What if we kicked a soccer ball every minute all night long? We’d throw a huge party, keep the stadium lights on, and invite the whole town to come watch a collective dream come true. » Roderick Buchanan

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes

I’d really like to know.

What if we adopted this ceremony for ourselves?
