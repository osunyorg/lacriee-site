Inkjet print, 160 x 113 cm


« The Ball and the Stadium. Every time I watched a game from the stadium stands, I wished that one day the big defender would kick the ball over the roof and send it flying off toward the city. I’ve been dreaming about this for a while now. But I’d like to make that dream come true—only the other way around. Imagine a mortar that could fire balls onto the field from outside the stadium. A big, friendly cannon parked in the parking lot. Imagine the rumors spreading through the city. David versus Goliath. Is that even possible? At dusk, as the light fades and Stade Rennais transforms into a huge, empty shell on the outskirts of town, the first ball flies through the sky, clears the concrete wall, and lands right on the kickoff spot. Splash! » Roderick Buchanan

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
