Jocelyn Cottencin created Untitled (Just a Walk ), a series of three photographs that focus our attention on people engaged in a concrete physical action—such as walking—or, conversely, lost in a state of mental and physical abandonment. Action here is defined not in terms of purpose (the possibility of a fictional narrative is left to the viewer) but rather in terms of flow and rhythm. The use of the light box serves to “reflect” the image—which is outside the frame and out of context—of the places captured in the photograph. The diffusion of the image through light interacts with the visitor’s other ways of moving through the exhibition. It significantly softens and alters the established boundaries and points of reference. At the heart of Jocelyn Cottencin’s creative process is the relinquishment of control over the gaze in an attempt to create images that already contain something other than what they show: another place, another temporality… Beyond the contemplation they initially inspire, Jocelyn Cottencin’s works elude us. Often imposing but never spectacular, they leave room for doubt and an uncertain perception. Behind the fluidity of situations, the slowness of a movement, and the roundness of a form, a critical stance emerges that opposes any form of easy seduction. Larys Frogier

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
