Aline Morvan

body: >-


synthetic plaster, 770 x 490 x 3.2 cm


In her work, Aline Morvan explores the concepts of urban space, cartography, and narrative. Straddling the line between reality and fiction, she questions and subverts the landmarks that define the urban environment and develops the idea of an imaginary journey through mental projections. For La Criée, Aline Morvan created a monumental piece composed of small building-block modules, cast in white marble-like plaster. Titled Dessine moi une maison (Draw Me a House ), Aline Morvan’s work blends into and reveals itself on one of the walls of La Criée: the design is visible as a whole—like the archetype of a house in the collective imagination—but also in detail, revealing the imperfections and irregularities of the casting and handcrafted production. Because of the choice of material—which is extremely fragile—the small structural elements lose their modular nature and become a single, unified piece—somewhere between drawing, sculpture, and architecture.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes

Aline Morvan, Dessine moi une maison , vue de l'exposition Risk , La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes, 2008  © Benoît Mauras
