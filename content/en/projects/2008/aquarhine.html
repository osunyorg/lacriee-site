diving mask, glass, latex


In early 2007, as part of the 4th International Polar Year, Laurent Duthion was invited by the French Polar Institute to take part in an expedition and stay at a research station in Adélie Land (Antarctica). He conducted research on animal and mineral odor samples, which led to his work on developing products for aquatic environments and the creation of the Aquarhine; This is a diving mask to which a snorkel has been attached at the nose, allowing the user to use their mouth freely underwater while breathing—enabling them, in particular, to eat, drink, or smell through retro-olfaction underwater. Laurent Duthion enlisted Nicolas Hadjitournos to test this third prototype of the Aquarhine and conduct an underwater tasting in the aquarium set up in the exhibition space. The video shown documents this initial experiment conducted at La Criée, which was repeated in front of a public audience during the opening reception.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
