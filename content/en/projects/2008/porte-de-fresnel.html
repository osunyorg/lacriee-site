door, Fresnel lens, glass, 83 x 4 x 205 cm


Between two exhibition halls, visitors are invited to pass through the Fresnel Door: this door, which appears ordinary in size and appearance, features a giant Fresnel lens in its upper half. While retaining its usual function, the door becomes an optical device capable of creating spatial illusions—such as a temporary exaggeration of perspective or a reversal of it… The Fresnel lens was originally invented for use in the optical systems of marine navigation lights, but it is also found in video projectors to convert the image from the internal screen into an enlarged image.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
