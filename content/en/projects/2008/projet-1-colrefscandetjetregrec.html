Julien Duporté

body: >-


wood, paper, markers, stamps, scanner, basketball hoops, computer, 770 x 330 x 500 cm


Julien Duporté takes a somewhat ironic look at the concept of work, the status of the artist, and the connections between the art world and everyday life. For La Criée, he conceived the project #01 COLREFSCANDETJETREGREC (color/reference/scan/destroy/throw away/look/start over), which explores the relationship between work and play.Throughout the exhibition, Julien Duporté endlessly repeats the same gestures reminiscent of those little games people play to escape the daily grind of school or work. Systematic repetition takes away the fun of throwing paper balls—success or failure no longer matters—and the playful activity becomes a task to be accomplished with a productive goal: the repeated gesture gradually creates a shape and alters the space as the paper balls accumulate on the floor. It is the rigor inherent in the absurdity of production methods that gives the play its form and concept. By stubbornly repeating these gestures to the point of exhaustion, Julien Duporté highlights the “risk” we face today: that of forgetting all those little moments that allow us to escape from everyday life.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
