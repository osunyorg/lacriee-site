aquarium


For Jean Moulin School, Laurent Duthion conceived a project titled The Possibility of a Mermaid . He invited the students to take part in a process of research and creation, in line with his approach that bridges the arts and sciences. During the workshops, the students conducted research through drawings, collecting objects, and writing texts in an effort to imagine the evolution of species and humans in an underwater environment. At the end of the artist’s residency, the students collaborated with the artist to create an original piece: an “aquaterrarium” that blends the sensory, the living, and the imaginary.

Partners : -- Académie de Rennes / DSDEN35 -- École Jean Moulin, Rennes

Support : -- Ministère de la Culture / Drac Bretagne -- Ville de Rennes / DEE
