Estrella Estevez

body: >-


charcoal, resin, 400 x 50 cm


Estrella Estevez creates sculptures inspired by the opposing forces that make up nature. Her various concrete castings (crumpled sheet metal, hewn tree trunks, cobblestones) reveal on their surfaces a clash of materials that emerges in a floating manner, whether in their forms or their modes of presentation. This tension between the raw, heavy material and the ethereal quality of the surfaces lends his sculptures a dramatic dimension, emphasized by the dark hues of the materials used. The piece created for La Criée features an oversized piece of charcoal. Carbon thus replaces limestone, and this interplay of inverted materials and colors contributes to the ambivalence between solidity and fragility, resistance and collapse, persistence and erasure, and so on. The oversized chalk accentuates the density and sense of mass of the raw material, in contrast to the “white cube” of the exhibition space.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
