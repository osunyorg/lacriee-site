Gianni Motti

body: >-


t-shirt, assistants


The second work created by Gianni Motti as part of his “Think Tank” exhibition is the deployment of a Gianni Motti Assistant who wanders through the city of Rennes wearing a yellow T-shirt bearing the words “Gianni Motti Assistant” in black letters. This resulted in several photographs, including one that was widely distributed by mail, through public displays, and on the internet.  The photograph depicts the physical and textual encounter between the “Gianni Motti Assistant” and the “Municipal Police.” The Gianni Motti Assistant(s) series began in 1997, when Sébastien Pecques, a young graduate of the Grenoble School of Fine Arts, received a grant to work for six months with a renowned artist. He chose Gianni Motti, who suggested that he travel around the world until his grant ran out, with the sole requirement that he wear a T-shirt bearing the “Gianni Motti Assistant” logo.Since then, Motti's assistants have multiplied all over the world, in both the West and the East. Today there are 1,402 of them, and they can be found among the protesters in Paris, Davos, Genoa, Seattle, and Rennes; in the pages of various newspapers; on a street in Tehran; or on an island in the Pacific… Through this process, Gianni Motti questions the value of artistic production—which has been delegated and dematerialized within the flow of media information. The appearances of Gianni Motti’s Assistant(s) in all corners of the world activate the artist’s presence without revealing the mission assigned to them: to signal, to interact according to the context, and to engage with the environment as an Assistant. Carole Brulard

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
