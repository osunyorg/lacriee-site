bicycle, 16 mm camera, tent, mattress, panniers, 180 x 70 x 101 cm


Laurent Duthion’s 2007 trip to Antarctica led him to reflect on humanity’s aquatic future, the history of evolution as it relates to marine environments, and the myth of mermaids… But it also allowed him to physically experience life and the challenges of the polar environment. The work Bolex-Mobile is a testament to this. It consists of a 16-mm Bolex camera mounted on a bicycle so that its operation and the capture of images depend on the rotation of the front wheel. The artist adapted the principle developed for La Caméra musculaire in 2005, in collaboration with Rectif + (a grinding workshop in Rennes), so that the Bolex-Mobile could be ridden on relatively flat and unconventional surfaces (such as ice, snow, or sand). In February 2008, Laurent Duthion traveled to the fjords of Spitsbergen (an island in the Arctic Ocean) to undertake a solo expedition and test the Bolex-mobile at the end of the polar night. The film Sunfest , screened at La Criée, captures in images a day from this journey across the Arctic ice floes. Sunfest , which means “sun festival” in Norwegian, thus highlights the landscape’s play of light. The acceleration of the bicycle’s speed is reflected in the image as underexposure, while deceleration results in overexposure of the film. In this work, muscular energy is harnessed to produce a moving image that tends toward abstraction.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
