body: >-


color video, sound, 6 min. 40 sec.


Les Grands Projets , is a series of short video films depicting simple actions and situations, created with the help of extras. A nod to ambition, to the undefined space between power and possibility, and to the clouds of uncertainty, the series *Les grands projets* is permeated by the theme of portraiture. Les Grands Projets n°1 (Bricolage)  is a filmed performance featuring the physical participation of the Art to be team, organizers of Les Ateliers de Rennes—Contemporary Art Biennial. At the artist’s suggestion, the Art to be team took part in a DIY project: building and tearing down a cinder block wall. Prior to the opening of the Biennale exhibitions, Les Ateliers de Rennes will host this video project, which will be shown continuously on the facade of the Jacobins Convent on Rue de Saint-Malo in Rennes from April 30 to May 16, 2008. BRICOLAGE subst. masc. I. 1. The act of engaging in manual work, either as a pastime or to save money. An activity that requires ingenuity and skill. The joys of DIY. 2. Specifically: Construction and renovation work related to buildings, carried out outside a professional setting. II. 1. Used to describe an action carried out as best one can, with whatever means are available. 2. Metaphorically: a human activity of uncertain technical quality. Charlotte Blin
