LC_Miedinger versus Lick
LC_Fontana versus Maed
LC_Albers versus Rile
LC_Vertov versus Jud
LC_Lissitzky versus Brody


These five typefaces emerged from our work on the new visual identity for La Criée, a contemporary art center. For
this project, our approach explores the evolution of this art
center-from the "original white cube" to a space that can no longer be
defined solely as an exhibition venue. We draw a close analogy by
exploring what the concept of the "white cube" might mean in the context
of graphic design.  Helvetica stands out as the obvious choice. Much
like the art center, which develops initiatives that extend far beyond
the gallery's walls, the typographic grid-based on Helvetica-becomes a
playground for contrasting graphic experiments (versus). The
typefaces created for La Criée deconstruct one reality to represent
another-one that is complex and multifaceted. Their names evoke our
"madeleines," chimeras, and ghosts. Jocelyn Cottencin & Richard Louvet [Lieuxcommuns]

These five typefaces emerged from our work on the new visual identity for La Criée, a contemporary art center.

For this project, our approach explores the evolution of this art center-from the "original white cube" to a space that can no longer be defined solely as an exhibition venue.
We draw a close analogy by exploring what the concept of the "white cube" might mean in the context of graphic design. 
Helvetica stands out as the obvious choice.

Much like the art center, which develops initiatives that extend far beyond the gallery's walls, the typographic grid-based on Helvetica-becomes a playground for contrasting graphic experiments (versus).
The typefaces created for La Criée deconstruct one reality to represent another-one that is complex and multifaceted. Their names evoke our "madeleines," chimeras, and ghosts.

Jocelyn Cottencin & Richard Louvet [Lieuxcommuns]
