Approximately 17,000 loose pavers laid on the ground area of 160 m²


For his exhibition Think Tank at La Criée, Gianni Motti created a radical installation within the art center: nearly 17,000 unconnected paving stones cover the floor of La Criée. The absence of joints creates a floating floor and lends a sense of instability to visitors as they walk through the space. Both the artwork and the audience are thus walking a tightrope. At once dense, solid, yet also understated, precarious, and slightly unsteady, the installation raises various contemporary issues, such as a lack of commitment and pervasive uncertainty. The work plays on the paradox between the emptiness of the walls—which creates a sense of vacancy—and the density of the cobblestones on the floor, revealing, through a sensitive approach, the issue of the decline of critical thinking. The installation also explores the role of the artist and that of the art center within the concept of the value of labor. Indeed, Gianni Motti’s artistic approach is rooted in discussion and inquiry rather than in unbridled productivity. This installation is a brilliant example of this: echoing the events of May 1968, it is not a work of remembrance or activism, but rather a work that poses a question, in which the artist employs symbolic and conceptual twists to reignite the power of critical thinking. With his exhibition Think Tank , Gianni Motti challenges the routines of visitors and the art center staff who are supporting him in this adventure. For the first fifteen days of the exhibition, La Criée was open 24 hours a day. During this period, the La Criée staff was called upon to maintain, at the artist’s request, a “minimum service” level for their daily activities. From June 6 to July 20, the art center will resume its normal work schedule and regular opening hours. Gianni Motti thus invites us to reframe the concept of productivity in favor of fostering critical thinking, within an exhibition whose title—not without irony—refers to corporate management. Think Tank is, in fact, a term used to describe “think tanks” or “reservoirs of thought” where opinion leaders (business executives, politicians, etc.) come together to share their ideas. Carole Brulard

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
