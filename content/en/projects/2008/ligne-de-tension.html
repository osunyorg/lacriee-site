steel cable, lifting rings, hook, 875 x 15 cm


In her work, Claire Daudin explores our relationship to time and space. She uses drawing and video to document reality or to recreate from memory the elements she perceives as she moves through urban spaces. The concepts of transformation, habitat, precariousness, and generative movements are at the heart of her work. As part of the exhibition Risk , Claire Daudin presents a minimalist suspended architectural piece Ligne de tension which consists of a taut cable that traces a diagonal line through space, connecting the floor to the beams of La Criée’s structural framework. The work traverses the exhibition space as a visual sign that functions as much as a strike-through, an anchor, and a support. It blends states of perception that oscillate between suspension, heaviness, lightness, and slippage. Ultimately, it disrupts the perception of the other works in the exhibition, for wherever the visitor stands, they must contend with this line materialized within the spindly space. This radical act is the result of a process highlighted in the small room at La Criée: the video Tribulations au sac à dos features a character strapped into an oversized backpack that serves as his makeshift shelter as he makes his way through urban spaces. The images emphasize the backpack’s resistance to collapse and disappearance, which is made possible by movement. The series of drawings  Suspended Architecture  sketches the mental images of this journey through the city and the process of distilling the forms and lines revealed by the taut cable. The views appear still, frozen in time, captured by a swift and incisive stroke that highlights the balance of the collective urban fabric.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
