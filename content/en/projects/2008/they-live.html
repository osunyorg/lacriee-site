mural, 3 oil and acrylic paintings on canvas 160 x 300 cm each painting on canvas 


 Julien Quentin’s paintings draw on a variety of influences: classical, Pop, and media-based. The piece They Live! , presented at La Criée, consists of an acrylic mural punctuated by fleurs-de-lis in shades of blue, white, and red, along with three paintings on canvas. Here, Julien Quentin plays with symbols that have been reappropriated and reimagined in a contemporary social context. These become purely decorative elements on a wall panel, reminiscent of advertising spaces. The paintings of lilies serve as a backdrop for a contemplative triptych, which ironically echoes classical paintings. 
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
