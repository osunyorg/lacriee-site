body: >-


100 wooden frames, 100 black-and-white photocopies, broken glass, 59.4 x 42 cm per frame


Breaking Icons  (Breaking Icons) features portraits of intellectuals, artists, and politicians who have had a decisive and constructive influence on Jota Castro’s life. The artist shattered the glass frames with a hammer. Somewhere between admiration and rejection, this act also challenges our temptation to recognize these portraits. The work thus becomes a metaphor for the symbolic violence involved in freeing ourselves from the fascination our idols hold over us so that we can make our own choices.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes

courtesy galerie Massimo Minini, Brescia

Jota Castro,
