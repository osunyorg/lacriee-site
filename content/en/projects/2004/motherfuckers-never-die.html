Jota Castro

body: >-


3 light boxes, 180 x 120 cm each


Motherfuckers never die  : Three light boxes in different colors display lists of people and institutions that, according to Jota Castro, hold controversial positions. The purple box lists the companies and multinational corporations that control the global economy. The red box is a subjective list of politicians and intellectuals. The black box lists the names of Islamist suicide bombers in the Middle East. For each unit—presented as a list or a meeting seating chart—Jota Castro used the official United Nations typeface, creating ambiguous juxtapositions.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes

courtesy galerie Massimo Minini, Brescia
