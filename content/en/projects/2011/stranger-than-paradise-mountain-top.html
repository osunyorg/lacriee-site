200 ceramic sculptures, 200 wooden bases, varying dimensions


The installation Stranger than Paradise, Mountain Top , presented in the main exhibition space, consists of 200 ceramic sculptures placed on pedestals of varying heights, depicting animals in mating positions. Each pair combines different species (for example, an elephant and a tiger, a stork and a puma, a wolf and a monkey), thereby defying physical peculiarities and incongruities.

Production : -- La Criée centre d’art contemporain, Rennes -- galerie Jeanne Jaeger Bucher, Paris courtesy de l’artiste et galerie Jaeger-Bucher
