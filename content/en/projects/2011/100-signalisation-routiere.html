1 text, 6 photographs, each measuring 17.8 x 23.4 cm



Mircea Nicolae weaves his own emotional narrative through gestures so small and poetic that it’s impossible to describe them with grand words. As he gradually lets you into his deeply intimate subjects, you’re drawn in by the honesty of the introspective fiction he creates. He simply lays bare his loneliness, his love, and his sensitivity—and for that, you owe it to him to set him free…. Realizing his freedom also allows you to recognize his ways of producing and validating Truth and Goodness as being autonomous and consistent in and of themselves. Then, when you align yourself with his morality (the Archimedean point that makes Mircea Nicolae John Boyd’s future warrior) and surrender to it for a moment, the collapse of your intellectual framework makes a profoundly ethical encounter possible. Taking a reflective approach, Nicolae’s interventions address the themes of public space, power structures, and the revolutionary potential of art. This is the most effective way to bring about revolutions. “100” is a series of interventions created by the artist, mostly in Bucharest. He had originally planned to create only 100 interventions, but as he neared the end, he discovered that it wasn’t easy to stop… “The number 100 has become anything, anywhere, anytime—which allows me to end the series while still continuing it,” he writes on his website. His most recent goal is “to keep doing this for another 10 years… starting now.”


Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
