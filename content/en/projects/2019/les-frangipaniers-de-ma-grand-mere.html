frangipani shoot, letter




The frangipani sapling, sent to La Criée from Los Angeles, comes with a letter telling its story. These plants, native to Central and South America, have traveled through time and acclimated to Asia and other parts of the world. For David Horvitz, they are now “full-fledged residents of Los Angeles.” The frangipani cuttings were given to him by his grandmother, whose family is originally from Japan. David Horvitz spreads them throughout the world as he travels and exhibits his work. In this way, he carries a piece of his history with him and entrusts his hosts with the task of cultivating them in turn. The work embodies the idea of transmission, while also evoking migration, putting down roots, and dispersion.



Courtesy de l'artiste, de ChertLüdde, Berlin et de Yvon Lambert Libraire & Éditeur
