Yoan Sorin et Florian Sumi

body: >-


HD video, color, sound, 6 min 44 sec


The film Das Land – Les Idiots is a fragment of a fictional story set on an island. A group of four young men, all dressed in white and equipped with electronic amulets, spend their time repeating actions that seem absurd at first glance but follow their own logic. The group, living in a community, appears organized, with its own rituals, dances, and customs. The men search for resources, dig, and find discarded computer parts, which they sell at a marketplace. Computer devices—archaeological relics or talismans—become a medium of exchange with an unknown outside world. These fools live in the present, outside of time. They make do with what’s at hand, to the rhythm of a very old Italian song. It is the “Secondo Coro Delle Lavandai” (Second Chorus of the Washerwomen), a song originating in Puglia, which was featured in the Neapolitan musical fable La Gatta Cenerentola (Cinderella the Cat) in 1976. In fact, the cat seems to have left a mark on one of the idiots' pants. The film takes the form of an installation, with a few electronic charms and amulets hanging from the screen. In this way, the artists reveal various patterns that connect us to the world, and it is up to us to determine their meaning.

Featuring : Paul Brunet, Damien Moreira, Yoan Sorin et Florian Sumi Music : -- Nuovo Compagnia di Canto -- Popolare Secondo Corro Delle Lavandale Courtesy of the artists of galerie Valeria Cetraro, Paris
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
