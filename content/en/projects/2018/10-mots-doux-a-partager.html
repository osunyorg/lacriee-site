10 A5 flyers assorted pastel colors 1,000 copies free



Publish words that are pleasant to read and hear, so we can share them. Publish plenty of them so everyone can take some and post them on the Guillaume Régnier Hospital’s website. For the exhibition, decorate the walls with these gentle words and give away the remaining ones to continue spreading a little tenderness beyond the hospital walls.


Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes

Supports : -- ministère de la Culture / Drac Bretagne -- ministère de la Santé / ARS Bretagne Partner : -- centre hospitalier Guillaume Régnier, pôle G04, Rennes
