peinture sur textile de Flora Moscovici, performance et audioguide de Lina Schlageter


For an event on July 7, 2018, Flora Moscovici invited choreographer Lina Schlageter to interact with the landscape and its colorful installations through gestures, postures, and movements. She also created an audio track to listen to during the walk (downloadable at www.soundcloud.com/laga-t/sets/certaines-peintures), which traces the route through spoken or sung commentary and conversations with other participants.



Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes Partners : -- Les Ateliers du Plessix-Madeuc -- musée Yvonne-Jean Haffen -- communauté de communes de Dinan -- Lefranc Bourgeois et Sennelier Support : -- ministère de la Culture - Drac Bretagne
