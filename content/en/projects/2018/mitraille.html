Estelle Chaigne

body: >-


Velvia 50 film sheets, 40x75 cm and 20x25 cm, light boxes


Following a series of photographic experiments conducted for Le Monde à l’envers  (a project exploring the correspondence between two school classes in Pléchatel and Siem Reap, Cambodia), Estelle Chaigne continues her research into the ways in which images emerge by firing a shotgun loaded with buckshot, riddling a roll of film placed inside a pinhole camera with impacts. The pellets pierce the slide film and cause the image to diffract. The subject is reproduced excessively, overlapping, multiplying, and becoming visible only in certain parts of the photograph. The image is unique, fragile, and irreproducible.

Supports : -- ministère de la Culture / Drac Bretagne -- dispositif « des clics et des classes », Réseau Canopé
