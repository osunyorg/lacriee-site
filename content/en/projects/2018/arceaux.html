lacquer on metal hoops




In the garden of the Yvonne Jean-Haffen Museum, Flora Moscovici created a lacquer painting on seventeen metal arches. This composition is designed in a color palette that closely matches the surrounding environment, highlighting the structure of the arches. It integrates them into the landscape while setting them apart through the light they emit.  The choice of these architectural elements is no coincidence: these same arches appear in certain paintings by Yvonne Jean-Haffen that are part of the museum’s collection.


Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes Partners : -- Les Ateliers du Plessix-Madeuc -- musée Yvonne-Jean Haffen -- communauté de communes de Dinan -- Lefranc Bourgeois et Sennelier Support : -- ministère de la Culture - Drac Bretagne
