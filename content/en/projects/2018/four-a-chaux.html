Flora Moscovici

body: >-


pigments and lime wash on a wall




As an artist-in-residence in Léhon, near Dinan, invited by La Criée and the Ateliers du Plessis-Madeuc as part of the Territoires EXTRA project, Flora Moscovici created several ephemeral works along the towpath between the Yvonne-Jean Haffen Museum and Léhon Abbey. In the museum’s gardens, she painted inside a 19th-century lime kiln that is believed to have been used in the construction of the Ille-et-Rance Canal. Flora Moscovici highlights this historic architecture and creates a dialogue with the vegetation growing there.



Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes Partners : -- Les Ateliers du Plessix-Madeuc -- musée Yvonne-Jean Haffen -- communauté de communes de Dinan -- Lefranc Bourgeois et Sennelier Supports : -- ministère de la Culture - Drac Bretagne
