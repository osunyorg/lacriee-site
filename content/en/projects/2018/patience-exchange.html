scarf



Patience Exchange  is a scarf I knitted to mark breaks (while I was knitting it) amid a frantic pace—a scarf made with intention but without focus, unfinished and full of holes, like memory, like moments of daydreaming. It has been loaned to the mediators: “You give me your time, I’ll give you mine—let’s take care of each other.” Once it’s returned, the time spent on display will have left its invisible mark on it.


Production : La Criée centre d’art contemporain, Rennes
