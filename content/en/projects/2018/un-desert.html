sound piece, 17 min


A Desert is a sound piece consisting of ten minutes of music and seven minutes of silence. Modular sounds recorded at the legendary EMS studios in Stockholm coexist with electronic drones, electroacoustic noises, human breathing, and stretches of silence. A desert is an ever-changing soundscape—sometimes barely perceptible, sometimes all-encompassing—somewhere between birdsong and the sound of an airplane; an ambient piece in the vein of Érik Satie’s Musiques d’ameublement or Brian Eno’s Music for Airports.

Production : La Criée centre d’art contemporain, Rennes Soutien : -- Elektronmusikstudion, Stockholm, -- Institut français -- région Bretagne
