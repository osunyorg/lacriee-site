paintings on textiles, paintings on canoes, performances


During an event on July 7, 2018, the art walk Certaines peintures se promènent featured numerous performances by collaborators the artist met during her residency: ponies wear colorful skirts, painted kayaks and canoes float down the Rance River, children wear capes they made with the artist, and yoga practitioners strike poses, dressed in clothing that has also been painted.



Production : La Criée centre d’art contemporain, Rennes Partners : -- Les Ateliers du Plessix-Madeuc -- musée Yvonne-Jean Haffen -- communauté de communes de Dinan -- Lefranc Bourgeois et Sennelier Supports : -- ministère de la Culture – Drac Bretagne
