pigments et eau chaude sur mur


In the gardens of Léhon Abbey, Floa Moscovici has taken over a historic building, the Gloriette, a former “monks’ wine cellar” built in the 18th century. For these works, the artist uses eco-friendly and ephemeral techniques: a mixture of pigments, water, and lime, as well as the tempera technique, which involves a mixture of eggs and pigments.



Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes Partners : -- Les Ateliers du Plessix-Madeuc -- musée Yvonne-Jean Haffen -- communauté de communes de Dinan -- Lefranc Bourgeois et Sennelier Supports : -- ministère de la Culture - Drac Bretagne
