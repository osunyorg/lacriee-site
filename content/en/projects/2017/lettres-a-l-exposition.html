body: >-


performances d'écritures et textes imprimés


Julien Bismuth offers a glimpse into his creative process through the Lettres à l'exposition . He plans to write ten of them—starting in Rennes, then across Europe, and finally in Brazil in August. The first three letters are written in public within the exhibition space. These improvised writings, addressed directly to an in-person or virtual audience, come to an end when the surface of the sheet of paper or computer screen is filled. These pages are then printed and displayed at La Criée.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
