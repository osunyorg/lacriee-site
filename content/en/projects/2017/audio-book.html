sound piece, 420 min











Audio Book  is a seven-hour sound piece inspired by the Canadian Environments
records of the 1970s, as well as those from the Folkways Library, which
invite listeners to explore different types of landscapes, atmospheres,
relaxation techniques, and educational content through sound, but also
by sound pieces by composers such as Robert Ashley, Delia Derbyshire,
and Beatriz Ferrara, as well as New Age musicians like Ariel Kalma and
Laraaji. It can be viewed as the soundtrack to the film being
screened at the same time, but it also has a life of its own. The film
and the sound piece are like two companions who have come together this
time, but who can also be experienced separately. Audio Book  a
mishmash, like a radio program full of glitches and disparate elements
that clash: Wikipedia descriptions of desert flora and fauna, poems
written while traveling, sound recordings from the Mojave Desert and
Brittany, modular sounds recorded at EMS Studios in Stockholm using
Buchla and Serge synthesizers, and whispered voices that are sometimes
shifted toward higher or lower pitches.  Audio Book is an
experience in which there is, in fact, no "Here" or "There" other than
the fleeting space of the listening and viewing room, where the viewer
is invited to willingly waste time, doze off, and dream. Some of the sounds from Audio Book also appear on the album released by Shelter Press, Hand in Hand.

sound piece, 420 min

- kind: block template: chapter ranks: base: 2 top: active: false data: layout: no_background text: >-

Audio Book is a seven-hour sound piece inspired by the Canadian Environments records of the 1970s, as well as those from the Folkways Library, which invite listeners to explore different types of landscapes, atmospheres, relaxation techniques, and educational content through sound, but also by sound pieces by composers such as Robert Ashley, Delia Derbyshire, and Beatriz Ferrara, as well as New Age musicians like Ariel Kalma and Laraaji.

It can be viewed as the soundtrack to the film being screened at the same time, but it also has a life of its own. The film and the sound piece are like two companions who have come together this time, but who can also be experienced separately.Audio Book a mishmash, like a radio program full of glitches and disparate elements that clash: Wikipedia descriptions of desert flora and fauna, poems written while traveling, sound recordings from the Mojave Desert and Brittany, modular sounds recorded at EMS Studios in Stockholm using Buchla and Serge synthesizers, and whispered voices that are sometimes shifted toward higher or lower pitches. Audio Book is an experience in which there is, in fact, no "Here" or "There" other than the fleeting space of the listening and viewing room, where the viewer is invited to willingly waste time, doze off, and dream.


Some of the sounds from Audio Book also appear on the album released by Shelter Press, Hand in Hand.
