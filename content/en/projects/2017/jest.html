body: >-


three color photographs


These photos were taken during the installation of an artwork in Bremen, Germany. I filled a bottle with water, then placed two tulips inside it. I then grabbed the tulips by their stems, let go of the bottle, and let it shatter on the ground. Only the broken pieces of the bottle and the spilled water were part of the installation.


Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
