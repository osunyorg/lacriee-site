Félicia Atkinson

body: >-


5 digital prints on brushed aluminum, 50 cm in diameter










The memoryless cards are one-of-a-kind prints on brushed aluminum in circular shapes, somewhere between a vinyl record, a mirror, and a magnified view through a telescope. These are digital transcriptions of collages I created in my sketchbooks, which were scanned and converted to black and white for the publication Audio Book , a prelude to the exhibition published in February 2017 by Shelter Press, the publishing house and record label I founded with Bartolomé Sanson. Here, the prints were once again scanned, colorized, and modified from the files used in the publication, transforming them back into original and unique prints: unlike the images in the book, these exist in only one copy. The brushed aluminum base evokes the texture of certain computers and cell phones—a metallic texture we’ve grown accustomed to touching every day without necessarily realizing it. Here, the digital and the analog intersect, offering a hybrid sensory experience in which the digital becomes tangible, the multiple becomes unique, and vice versa: worlds and surfaces are porous and sensitive spaces.












Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
