rubber mat, cash drawer, variable dimensions


The Dutch collective gerlach en koop also plays freely with associations, presenting a new iteration of Pillow Objects  : a cash drawer on a rubber mat and a rubber mat on a cash drawer.  The exhibited piece is part of a series that pairs two very different objects each time, positioned in a mirror image—one on top and the other on the bottom—whose juxtaposition gives rise to multiple interpretations.  and associations of ideas. The collective’s sculptural and conceptual approach involves modifying, moving, or duplicating ordinary objects to bring about formal and linguistic reversals. Pillow objects change from one exhibition to the next, but one of them always retains the memory of its predecessors. The rubber mat is a kind of “synthesis” of the most recent Pillow Objects , which combined a bag of potting soil on a coat rack and a coat rack on a bag of potting soil.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
