murals





Nos émotions  is a series of three murals whose forms consist of small, colorful Canson paper cutouts projected onto the wall on a large scale. The wall thus becomes a large, open page from a book or notebook. Painted in collaboration with three students from EESAB (Kahina Loumi, Margaux Janisset, and Alice Quentel), Nos émotions unfolds within the space of La Criée like a melody sung in harmony.











Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
