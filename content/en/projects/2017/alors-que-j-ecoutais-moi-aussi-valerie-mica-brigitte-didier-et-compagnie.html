audio text, 4 × 2 min


Drawing on interviews conducted by Sophie Kaplan with several librarians at the Kandinsky Library, Sophie Lapalu and Sophie Kaplan weave a dialogue in which the librarians’ experiences, memories, and convictions intertwine with their own memories and experiences as researchers, creating an implicit portrait of the Kandinsky Library through anecdotes and short stories that often intersect with the larger narrative, enriching it—and even putting it into perspective.



Thank you to Mica G., Didier S., Valérie G., and Brigitte V., as well as to the entire staff of the Kandinsky Library.
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
