Sound piece, 10 minutes 


Bérénice Klang is a fictional character whose initials are BK. One imagines that she would be a librarian and a musician, and that she would compose a sound piece for which the works in the Kandinsky Library could serve as a possible score. Reading while listening to inner voices.
F.A.
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
