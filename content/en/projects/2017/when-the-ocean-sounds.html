five posters from a series of fifty-one, black-and-white print, instructions


On the wall are five posters from a series of fifty-one, When the Ocean Sounds , which transcribe the sounds of the waves into onomatopoeia, accompanied by instructions. The piece is dedicated to Pauline Oliveiros, a leading figure in 1970s American minimalist music and electronic music. David Horvitz’s pieces were inspired in particular by his residency in Moncontour in the Côtes d’Armor in May 2017, at the invitation of La Criée for the Territoires EXTRA / The Great Learning project with Félicia Atkinson and Barbara Sirieix.

Courtesy l'artiste et ChertLüdde, Berlin Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
