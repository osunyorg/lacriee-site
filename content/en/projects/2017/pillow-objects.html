coat racks, all-purpose potting soil, various sizes



The Dutch collective gerlach en koop presents another set of associations, derived from ready-mades. Their sculptural and conceptual work involves modifying, moving, or copying ordinary objects through subtle, almost imperceptible gestures. Gerlach en koop makes these objects visible through repetition, copying, or reuse, as well as through displacement or inversion. The sculpture Pillow Objects is part of a series that pairs two very different objects in each installation; their juxtaposition gives rise to a multitude of interpretations, associations of ideas, and languages. Each piece consists of two pillows—objects on which one rests or reclines. For Gerlach en Koop, “the pillow means nothing in and of itself—or almost nothing—but it gives meaning to the object it supports.”  The “cushion” object changes from one exhibition to the next, but each piece retains the memory of the previous sculpture. The first Pillow Objects piece brought together two refrigerators and two chocolate bars displayed inside their respective industrial packaging; the second paired two pots of chocolate-colored paint with two soil-core sampling tools; the third Pillow Objects piece, on display at La Criée, consists of “a bag of potting soil on a coat rack and a coat rack on a bag of potting soil.” » ( a bag of soil on top of a cloakroom rack and a cloakroom rack on top of a bag of soil ). Between the two exhibitions, the sculpture will change (at least once, or even more, depending on how many times it is exhibited). The future Pillow Objects , which will be presented at La Criée in December 2017, will therefore be neither quite the same nor quite different.


Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
