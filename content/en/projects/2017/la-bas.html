painted metal shelf, plexiglass, 300 x 230 x 260 cm




Là-bas   is a hybrid sculpture-shelf, whose title draws—among other possible references—on Huysmans’s eponymous work*. Light and expansive, like a line drawn in space out of green metal, it may bring to mind a cactus, an elephant, or an abstract form. Placed on these Plexiglas shelves are small “instant” sculptures made of raw clay and various other materials, created as improvisations or impromptus, some of which have been enlarged and transformed on a very large scale. The curved line of the shelf interacts with the murals and the two other large-scale sculptures in the main room. This shelf plays on these questions of scale and the desires of both the artist and the viewer—the distinction between what is attainable and what is not, what one can and cannot touch. The piece stands stable, yet also appears fragile, ready to sway at the slightest breeze. Like a plant, a flower, or a mineral, Là-bas remains “available” in all the ambiguity that this implies: of course, one can pluck a petal from a flower—it’s “possible”—but that doesn’t mean one necessarily has to do it. It is precisely this sense of uncertainty that *Là-bas* seeks to convey.









* Joris-Karl Huysmans, Là-Bas , roman, 1891
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
