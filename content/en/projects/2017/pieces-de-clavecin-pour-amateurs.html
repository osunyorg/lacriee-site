French Hemsch-model harpsichord kit with two keyboards 235 x 320 x 250 cm


A Hemsch-model double-manual harpsichord kit is on display in pieces, as if awaiting possible assembly. The assembly instructions, meanwhile, are on display in the office of the associate artists. Once the preserve of an elite “niche,” the harpsichord became more widely accessible starting in 1954 with the kit invented by Zuckermann, which allowed anyone to build, decorate, and maintain one.


Special thanks to Emmanuel Danset and Marc Ducornet (The Paris Workshop,
Conflans Saint-Honorine)
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
