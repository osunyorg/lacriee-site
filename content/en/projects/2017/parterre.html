body: >-


6 chestnut logs, approx. 40 x 450 x 40 cm each


Parterre , a term used to describe the seating area in front of the stage; here, it refers to a bench composed of six rough-hewn wooden blocks. These are logs that have been cut into parallel slabs and reassembled by placing spacers between each slab to facilitate the drying of the wood—a technique commonly found in carpentry shops and harpsichord-makers’ workshops. The various types of wood found in the concert hall echo those used in the instrument’s construction. Since the woods vary by region (Italy, France, the Netherlands, etc.) and by musical or ornamental component (oak or beech for the structure—the back and sides; spruce for the soundboard, etc.), each harpsichord produces a unique tone.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
