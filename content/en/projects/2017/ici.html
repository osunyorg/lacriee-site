plaster, wood, 180 x 100 x 480 cm






Ici  is also a slightly distorted replica of a small, impromptu sculpture I made a few months before the exhibition. Just as with Ami(e) and Là-Bas , I asked the stage managers at La Criée—Benoît Mauras, Anthony Glais, and Pascal Moreul—to create a “giant miniature” of the small sculpture, which I then enhanced with stems, colors, and so on. In vast landscapes (the Grand Canyon, the Mojave Desert, etc.), as in memory, the realm of dreams, or narrative, scales sometimes seem difficult to grasp—and are even variable at times. A Chinese chopstick used in a small-scale sculpture becomes, in its enlarged version, the “Cockaigne pole” of a rock in the colors of chewing gum.  Ici  proposes to put this notion of “here”—where landscapes shift so slowly that they seem fixed, while people and animals will always be on the move—into perspective. There is no fixed “here,” but rather a multitude of “heres”—places of transition, welcome, and passage.









Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
