body: >-


PVC dance mat, wood floor, 496 x 496 cm


The stage, set to host the harpsichords for the recital, is titled “Dancefloor”—a nod to Chambonnières’s talents as a dancer and to the physicality of the piece, which consists of a black-and-white dance floor divided into a checkerboard pattern. The checkerboard pattern is also a recurring motif in 17th-century painting, particularly in the works of Vermeer. It can be found, for example, in The Concert (1664), which depicts a harpsichord, and in The Art of Painting (1666).

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
