wall painting, acrylic


Some artists have chosen to add a new chapter to the stories they began last winter. Canadian artist Zin Taylor reveals what has happened over the past year to the figures and forms he drew on the walls of the art center, transforming some of them into three-dimensional sculptures. His drawings, sculptures, performances, and prints draw as much from popular culture (particularly the underground music scene) as they do from art history. Here, the artist projects her voice into the space of La Criée to tell us The Story of Lines and Points , which inspires her drawings: “A point in space is a unit of sound” […] “that transforms into a line” […] “These are the waves of time vibrating. Listen to the electric flow of their movement.” These words resonate with Daphne Oram’s Oramics and David Horvitz’s sounds of waves: by splitting into two, the narratives of each work expand the range of possible interpretations of the exhibition.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
