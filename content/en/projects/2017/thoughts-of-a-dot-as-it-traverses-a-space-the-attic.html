dessin mural, acrylique, 770 x 776 cm


Zin Taylor has created a new work titled Thoughts of a dot as it traverses a space (The Attic) or La pensée d’un point traversant un espace (Le Grenier) . The artist creates installations that blend performance, sculpture, drawing, printmaking, and video. Her work is primarily narrative in nature, taking the form of stories that draw as much from popular culture (particularly the underground music scene) as from contemporary art.  Sharing a fascination with language with many artists of his generation, Zin Taylor explores the connections between words and images through publications and artist’s books, which he typically releases to accompany his exhibitions. Across an entire wall at La Criée—highlighting the venue’s “loft-like” architecture—Zin Taylor created a mural inspired by his travels and his reading and the correspondence between the performance artists John Cage and Merce Cunningham in the 1970s.
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
