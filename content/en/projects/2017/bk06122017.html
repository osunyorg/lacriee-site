François Bonnet

body: >-


sound piece, 20 min


I need my books. At all times. They are the cornerstones of my imagination and memory—once I’ve read them, I’ve externalized them. As a result, I rarely visit libraries, since I’ve been fortunate enough to build my own, tailored to my reading capacity and my aspirations. For me, a library is less a place of study, sharing, and community than a sanctuary where I rediscover a piece of what I’ve built for myself. It’s a kind of map on which I plot my path. The piece thus lists the matching references between my library and the Kandinsky Library. F.B.
