chalk paint, fabric, 145 x 402 cm







I’m Following You is a colorful oil-chalk painting on a four-meter-long piece of pink cotton fabric—somewhere between a tablecloth, a curtain, a canvas, and a sheet. It is an unfolding narrative, an abstract story, a testament to an emotional temporality. It bridges the gap between the white room and the dark room. I’m Following You is also the title of the opening track on Félicia Atkinson's new album,  Hand in Hand , published by Shelter Press in May 2017.









Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
