body: >-


(with the help of Nico Dockx)


From its discovery in the BK’s collection on March 4, 2006, alongside Didier Schulmann, to its launch into the Mediterranean Sea ten years later as part of the exhibition Avec le vent , organized by Nico Dockx, the chronicle of the events that marked the eventful journey of the cover for the Feux Pâles exhibition catalog is recounted in a specially designed publication distributed to visitors at the evening event. The twists and turns of the story are linked to various works and documents from the BK’s collections, particularly the Philippe Thomas collection. They are on display for the occasion in the library’s display cases.
Remerciements à Alain Cavro, Staccato, Nantes
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
