Julien Bismuth

body: >-


screen print, screen prints on paper, 770 x 500 cm


This design was created from a sketch of an image I saw in a dream. One night, I was awakened by this image, which I then sketched—in the dark—in the notebook I always keep on my nightstand. The note that goes with it reads, “Caspar covers his eyes with his fingers.” I remember the image of a ghost in a white robe wearing a thick mask, with two fingers pressed into the eye holes of the mask.  I also remember the sense of urgency to wake up that this image instilled in me. I wrote “Caspar” with Casper the Friendly Ghost in mind.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
