Pierre Paulin

body: >-


Nike box, dubplate, 27 x 27 x 4.5 cm


Pierre Paulin, for his part, has developed a practice in writing and art based on the combination and translation of cultural formats and signs. The 1986 work Complexe n°4, #2 echoes the rotogravure cylinders exhibited previously. In it, the artist had engraved a text of his own composition about the anxiety caused by buying a pair of sneakers, alongside an image of an album by the group Run DMC, the creators of the song My Adidas . The work on display here offers a variation: it consists of a dubplate—an acetate vinyl record engraved with a piece of music—housed in a box made from a pair of athletic shoes’ packaging. This record is intended to be played at a party so that people can wear out their sneakers while dancing to a musical remix. This piece was created using the soundtrack from a video by Pierre Paulin titled Complexe de 1986 n°4, Conversation with the Voice of the Poem “Boom boom, run run *, which offers a personal account of the cultural history of the sneaker—a mass-produced consumer item and a means of expressing a unique style.


* Boom Boom Run Run  is also the title of the solo exhibition by Pierre Paulin, presented at the Plateau, Frac
Île-de-France du 20.09 au 17.12.17
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
