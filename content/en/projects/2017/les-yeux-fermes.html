Julien Bismuth et Virginie Yassef

body: >-


Julien Bismuth and Virginie Yassef create a book and its variations live, using a collection of images and texts they gathered at the Kandinsky Library and photographed with their cell phones over a five-year period. A selection of the photographed documents is laid out on a table, serving as the basis for a book that varies depending on the artists’ manipulations and the photocopier’s zoom settings.
