Yann Sérandour

body: >-


pair of antique armchairs without upholstery, 85 x 60 x 60 cm each framed pigment print, 34 x 51 cm



While it is possible to sit in the orchestra section of the concert hall without hearing the harpsichord, the listening lounge allows you to hear it—though you cannot sit in the armchairs, which have been stripped of their comfortable upholstery. Their title, Le Bon Coin (Khéops) , echoes their origins. Yann Sérandour found them on a website where individuals sell items online. While researching for the exhibition, he was surprised to discover a photograph of antique armchairs set against a checkered floor, with a dog appearing in the corner. By a happy coincidence, the artist chose to exhibit the photograph taken from the website—with the photographer’s permission—thus bringing the poster full circle.


Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes

Yann Sérandour, vue de l'exposition Pièces pour clavecin , La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes, 2017 © Benoît Mauras
