Yann Sérandour

body: >-


silkscreen print, oak frame, 178 x 121.5 cm 30 copies


On the wall of the main space, which has been transformed into a concert hall, a screen-printed poster announces the dates of the recital, featuring a program curated by Yann Sérandour and Pascal Dubreuil. Each recital includes a performance of one of the Pièces de Clavessin , two volumes published in 1670 by Jacques Champion de Chambonnières. To create the poster, Yann Sérandour enlisted the help of artist and graphic designer Jocelyn Cottencin. She combines the pink used in a previous work* by Yann Sérandour with shades of copper, gold, and silver, evoking the colors of the harpsichord’s iron, copper, and brass strings. As if recreating a color scheme from a musical score, he chose the same shade of pink to paint the wall at La Criée.


* L’Enseigne (How to Train Your Dog), 2015 Graphic Design : -- Jocelyn Cottencin -- Yann Sérandour Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
