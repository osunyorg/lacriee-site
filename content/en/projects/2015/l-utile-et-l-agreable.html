pontoon, sign, bench wood and stone, various dimensions


The series The Useful and the Pleasant brings together a signpost with no inscription, a wooden log dock, and a bench. Their forms are inspired by vernacular furniture, such as the signs found in North American national parks or the English commemorative benches placed in front of scenic viewpoints. These objects set the pace for the body and the gaze, inviting movement or rest, action or reading.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
