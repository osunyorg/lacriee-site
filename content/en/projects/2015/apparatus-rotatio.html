body: >-


soundtrack, daily-rotation turntable, screen-printed vinyl record



Dominique Blais’s Apparatus (Rotatio) captures the flow of a polar landscape by weaving connections between the visible and the invisible, between the audible and the inaudible. The installation presented at La Criée features a vinyl record screen-printed with a map of the Norwegian archipelago of Svalbard, rotating very slowly on a turntable, thereby separating the object from the sound being played. This work was created during a residency at the French Polar Institute in Ny-Ålesund, Norway, where the artist recorded the frequencies and variations of the magnetic field using a radio receiver and converted them into audible sound signals. Through this immersive installation, Dominique Blais invites us to listen to the raw sound emitted by a landscape and to explore the infra-ordinary. The installation reflects Dominique Blais’s approach, which seeks to represent the intangible, reveal phenomena that are usually imperceptible, and thereby sharpen our sensory perception. The artist becomes a conduit for the “all-natural magic of inaccessible forms.”



Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
