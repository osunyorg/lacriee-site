analog and digital photographs, 38 Lambda C-prints


From vast outdoor spaces, the exhibition takes us into intimate landscapes through the photographs of Olivier Roller. Known for his portraits of powerful men, he is exhibiting here for the first time a series created over fifteen years, largely on a property in the Cévennes,  Du moulinet et d’ailleurs (au dehors du dedans de moi). This series consists of “fragments of memory,” suspended moments in time, moments of boredom, family life, and memories—rearranged into a whole that plays on multiple levels, shifting from darkness to light, from tenderness to irony. The installation offers everyone the opportunity to view the photographs from up close or from a distance and to create their own narrative from selected fragments. For Olivier Roller, “Traveling through the countryside means reconnecting with our memories. These photographs capture the passing of the seasons in the Cévennes, where time slows down, where the senses come alive again, and where, by becoming a little more animal-like, I forget and step outside myself.”

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
