Abraham Poincheval

body: >-


chalk drawing on wood, model, 400 x 650 cm


For L’Épais Réel, the artist is presenting a work-in-progress titled Étude pour marcher sur la canopée nuageuse (Study for Walking on the Cloud Canopy) , in which he actually plans to walk on clouds. The artist develops novel ways of living—whether nomadic or sedentary—to experience the world from unexplored perspectives, sometimes pushing the limits of survival. His performance-based projects have led him to travel underground (a project with Laurent Tixador), to live inside the skin of a stuffed bear, to lock himself in a hole in the floor of a gallery, or on a high platform, come rain or shine. According to Ariane Michel, « Abraham Poincheval est au-delà du rêveur, il voit littéralement le rêve dans le réel  ».

Production : -- La Criée centre d’art contemporain, Rennes (troisième version) -- La seconde version de cette œuvre a été produite par Eternal Network pour Eternal Gallery à Tours en 2013.
