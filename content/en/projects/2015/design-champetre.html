video game


A video game project resulting from a workshop conducted with 7th- and 8th-grade classes at Angèle Vannier Middle School in Saint-Brice-en-Coglès. Drawing on their imagination, a selection of photos, and two poems by Raymond Queneau, the students designed objects on multiple scales to serve as content for this video game. Content creation and formatting were carried out independently to encourage experimentation at each stage.

Direction and Development : François Goujon
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
