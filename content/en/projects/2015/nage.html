HD color film, 2 min 33 sec



In it, we see Thomas Salvador performing a powerful, almost unreal move in the ocean. This video is made up of edited outtakes from the feature film.  Vincent n’a pas d’écailles* . In the film, Thomas Salvador plays Vincent—a man who gains superpowers when he comes into contact with water—without the use of digital effects. The footage shows him swimming, diving, doing the crawl, and the butterfly with ease in this element that has become second nature to him. The power and speed conveyed by the images invite viewers to enter the exhibition.



* Vincent n’a pas d’écailles  is a film directed and co-written by Thomas Salvador, which was released in theaters in France in February 2015. courtesy de l’artiste et de Christmas in July production
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
