body: >-


stereoscopic video with sound, 8 min 44 sec


We see models*—in the sense used by Robert Bresson—filmed in stereoscopy, rotating on their own axes. The busts seem to emerge from the frame and blend into the studio setting or the landscape in a series of metamorphoses. The film adopts a subjective and paradoxical point of view by combining, within the same image, the model and what he sees—the inner and outer gaze. Yves Chaudouët also plays with scale, projecting a set larger than the bust and revealing the inner workings of the installation—not without a touch of humor—as a tribute to Méliès and the early magicians of cinema. The soundtrack adds a new dimension by combining recordings captured in the Rennes countryside with the voice of Yann Boudaud, reciting passages from Essai la peinture .**
* « Modèle. Tu l’éclaires et il t’éclaire. La lumière
que tu reçois de lui s’ajoute à celle qu’il reçoit de toi. » Robert
