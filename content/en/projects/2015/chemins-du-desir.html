display case, silkscreen prints, documentary photographs, documents, video




The practice of reappropriating urban space and its motifs is also evident in the work of the Ripoulain Brothers. For La Criée, the duo Mathieu Tremblin and David Renault created Chemins du désir , a project that brings together a documented and filmed performance, screen-printed preparatory sketches, research, and “urban surveillance” photographs taken in the Blosne and Prairies Saint-Martin neighborhoods of Rennes. Paths of Desire are trails carved out in green spaces as residents pass through them, thereby tracing their daily routes—as a form of resistance to the rigidity of the map drawn up by urban planning. Drawing on a documented study of how these paths have evolved, the Ripoulain Brothers have developed three proposals for interventions to be implemented or revived. Step by step, in a forced march, the performative action consists of enriching an existing path of desire with poetic and non-functional extensions.



Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
