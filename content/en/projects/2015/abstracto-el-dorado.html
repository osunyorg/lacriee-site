painted wall covered with gold leaf, 400 x 350 cm


The Abtracto El Dorado golden wall evokes both abstraction and the myth of El Dorado. This myth, which emerged in the 16th century, refers to a land in South America where cities were built of gold. Reported by Spanish explorers, his quest fueled travelers’ tales and spurred the conquest of Latin America. Abstracto El Dorado also references the history of Latin American art by partially drawing on the title and aesthetic of the work Abstracto en dorado , created in 1968 by Mathias Goeritz at the Camino Real Hotel in Mexico City. Runo Lagomarsino's works play on anachronisms, the symbolism of elements, shifts in perspective, as well as poetry and language

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain
