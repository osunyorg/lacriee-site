According to Yves Chaudouët, “a painter’s style can be just as cinematic”*. The film La Mue illustrates the reflective nature of his approach. This fictional story follows the journey of an artist—played by actor Yann Boudaud—from the studio (a glassworks where crystal deep-sea fish are produced), through the countryside dotted with highways, commercial, and industrial areas, all the way to Naples.  Just as 18th-century painters traveled to Italy to study drawing and light, the artist embarks on a journey of self-discovery to return to the roots of seeing. “The landscape is no longer painted; it is walked,”** writes Yves Chaudouët. The film explores artistic production, the creative process, and its enduring relevance.
* Yves Chaudouët, Essai la peinture , édition Actes Sud, 2015, p. 80 * Idem, p.64 Co-production with Morphologie des Éléments
