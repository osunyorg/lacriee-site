oil on wood, 300 x 25 cm


« One of Redouani’s earliest pictorial approaches involves using rectangular wooden panels—very narrow and elongated—on which the painter works to fill the space with a gaunt, emaciated body depicted without any embellishment. The figure stands upright, facing the viewer, revealing its stark simplicity rather than the fullness of its flesh. It occupies the entire space of the painting but, paradoxically, does not impose any sense of overwhelming monumentality. Another intriguing aspect is the use of a dominant color, such as blue or red. Whether cool or warm, the color remains consistently pale due to a white undercoat that shines through the dominant color, further distancing the viewer from this overly cramped space. The aim is to create a vital space for the painting and the body in order to better resist an environment that prevents breathing and movement. » « This original approach to the figure evokes powerful echoes of art history. The first, as Redouani himself points out, is that of Rembrandt. The Flemish painter’s relevance lies in his ability to render skin tones through paint, whether in his self-portraits or in his scenes depicting the dissection of animal or human bodies. Rembrandt’s brushwork and mastery of chiaroscuro imbue the painted figure with a life that constantly surfaces and pulsates on the canvas. The reference to the Flemish painter may come as a surprise, since Redouani’s paintings do not feature this use of vibrating layers of paint. Painted in a single hue and perfectly smooth, the paint disembodies the body and confines it to a minimal space. However, Redouani invokes Rembrandt in his relentless quest to breathe life into his figures. When he speaks of his passion for chiaroscuro, he says he has not yet reached the light. The predominant use of darkness over light can be explained by the fact that death is still too omnipresent. »
Larys Frogier, « Peindre vivre peindre », in  Ouvertures algériennes, créations vivantes , exhibition catalog, Rennes : La Criée, centre d'art contemporain, 2003


Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
