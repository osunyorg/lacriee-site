« Alongside his narrow, vertical canvases, Redouani also creates paintings on wood, which are just as elongated but this time displayed horizontally. Here, it is no longer the entire body that is depicted, but rather a repetitive alignment of similar faces that fill the entire space of the painting. The narrowness of the painting no longer depends so much on its format as on the compression of the faces against one another. Varying in density, the arrangement of the faces differs from one painting to another, giving the impression of either extreme suffocation or barely any breathing. In Redouani’s painting, the ashen face bears no resemblance to the tortured expressiveness found in Edward Munch’s The Scream , where the mouth externalizes the fear that literally spills out of the canvas. Here, everything is contained within a latent anguish and an unbearable silence. ” In the Quran, “Al Mahshar” refers to the Plain of Gathering of Humanity on the Day of Judgment. « To better understand the significance of the face in Redouani’s works, one must in fact turn to the portraits by the Kabyle painter M’Hamed Issiakhem (1928–1985), a close friend of the writer Kateb Yacine. Indeed, Issiakhem painted faces whose aesthetic power does not lie in dramatic expressionism or realistic likeness. Devoid of specific identities, Issiakhem’s slender, gaunt faces contain a latent energy often mingled with sadness. Redouani’s work possesses that same power found in the humility of the face. However, while Issiakhem did indeed paint portraits of himself and others—his loved ones or public figures—it is impossible to describe Redouani’s faces as portraits. They are faces without any particular qualities other than the universality of inner life and melancholy. On the other hand, Issiakhem’s painting technique made extensive use of the effects of the paint itself, whereas Redouani’s technique resists a materiality that would already lend too much vitality to the faces. »
Larys Frogier, « Peindre vivre peindre », in  Ouvertures algériennes, créations vivantes, catalogue de l'exposition , Rennes : La Criée, centre d'art contemporain, 2003


Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
