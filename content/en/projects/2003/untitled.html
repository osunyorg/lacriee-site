photographic series mounted on aluminum, 3 pairs of elements: bodies + faces, 170 x 40 cm for each pair when combined


“Untitled” is a photographic series consisting of four life-size portraits of Algerian women. The intensity of the work is underscored by the fact that each head has been separated from its body. The viewer’s perception of the subject and ability to identify with her are further disrupted by the fact that these fragments are displayed facing each other. « Unable to return to Algeria during the twelve years of the “civil war,” I created a photographic project that directly addressed my frustration with the political situation, as well as the lack of coverage of this war in the British media. My motivation for creating this work can only be described as a literal expression of my feelings and as a reaction to the coverage of the war in England, which focused primarily on beheadings. The work is also an ironic reference to the French guillotine during the colonial era and, of course, to the history of the French Revolution. The idea of losing, severing a head, or decapitating a body is powerfully linked to the issue of anonymity and censorship—an iconoclastic act. Without a head, it is difficult to identify the body to which it belongs! By drawing on the personal and the political, the historical and the contemporary, Untitled , like many of my works, questions and rethinks icons and “rituals”. It is also a constant interplay between visibility and invisibility, disappearance and emergence, recognition and misunderstanding in the processes of identification. The conventional studio portrait has long been used to “capture” memory and to “fix” portraits in appealing depictions of the subject. In my work, I have translated this experience by erasing or blurring the details of the portrait, thereby negating or diminishing the “attractive” representation of the subject. »
Zineb Sedira, interview with Larys Frogier, Ouvertures algériennes, créations vivantes , Editions La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes, 2003


Production : -- La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes -- Courtesy galerie Kamel Mennour, Paris
