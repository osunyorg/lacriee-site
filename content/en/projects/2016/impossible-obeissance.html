copper, 1% gold, variable dimensions


Placed on the floor, the work consists of various metal assemblages. Their forms are inspired by plant and mineral motifs that the artist observed in nature and selected for their ability to evoke associations, much like symbols. The work is also the result of the alchemy of materials—an alloy of copper and gold heated to a very high temperature. During the melting process, the artist experienced the resistance of the gold, as part of it evaporated. This piece is a testament to Joana Escoval’s interest in chance, exploration, and the transformation of materials. It also reflects a free interpretation of nature’s forms. “I look for what seems unfinished or what is in motion—a substance that can exist at any time and in any place, in a sense without limits… *,” she says.



* Joana Escoval, interview, Mother Child – 2011, Halfhouse, Barcelone
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
