Revers  is a work created by Ismaïl Bahri for Les Ateliers de Rennes in co-production with La Criée. This time, the “capture device” is a film in which the same repetitive action plays out endlessly. A photograph on a magazine page, crumpled and smoothed out, disappears before our very eyes, while the ink from the page transfers onto the hands that alternately conceal and reveal the fleeting image to the viewer.

Co-production with Les Ateliers de Rennes, 2016
