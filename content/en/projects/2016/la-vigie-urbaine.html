performance


For a week, 24 hours a day, Abraham Poincheval lives inside a sculpture. Outside La Criée, in front of the art center’s entrance, at the top of a mast, a man keeps watch and gazes out over the city. « Perched on a platform measuring 1.90 meters long by 1 meter wide, I spend a week there completely self-sufficient. On this “raft of the peaks,” I am secured by a lifeline, as is all the equipment on board : a waterproof bag, a first-aid kit, meals for a week, two 9-liter jerry cans (one red, one white), a watertight canister, trash bags, a gas stove, cooking gear, two lighters, toilet paper, a change of clothes, a high-altitude sleeping bag, a rain cover for the sleeping bag, a rain poncho, a sleeping pad, a headlamp, a climbing harness, a lifeline, about ten carabiners, and a 13-meter rope. »
Abraham Poincheval For more than ten years, Abraham Poincheval has been devising traveling or stationary experiments to discover the world from previously unexplored perspectives. He crossed the Alps by pushing a metal cylinder that served as his vehicle, home, and camera obscura; he spent time underground twice in a space the size of his body; he lived inside a bear; inside a bottle; etc. La Vigie  was first presented in the summer of 2015 at Cap Sizun, facing the sea. Ariane Michel, an artist associated with the Fendre les Flots season, had invited it to participate in the group exhibition La Rhétorique des marées – Vol. 1 . In Rennes, the sound of traffic replaced that of the waves, and passersby took the place of hikers, and so on. A third version, titled Vigie / Stylite, was produced for the 2016 Nuit Blanche in Paris, in front of the Gare de Lyon.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
