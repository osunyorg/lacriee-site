Mioshe

body: >-


mural, 16 m


As part of the Fendre les flots  arts season, La Criée invited artist Mioshe to take up a residency in Saint-Malo to create an original work for public spaces. Committed to ocean conservation, the artist conceived Humano Plancton , a gigantic figure composed of hundreds of plankton drawings inspired by students from schools in Rennes and Saint-Malo. Whether through murals in public spaces or illustrations, Mioshe creates a surreal and colorful world populated by whimsical and fantastical figures, which here give way to the origins of life in the oceans. The infinitely small is treated as a graphic medium that can be at once versatile, minimalist, geometric, or highly exuberant. It is a rich source of formal creations, which the artist chooses to explore across various media for the Humano Plancton project (2015–2016): posters, as well as collages, murals in public spaces, and digital art on “canvas.”

Partner : Émeraude Habitation, office HLM public de Saint-Malo Support : ville de Saint-Malo Collaboration : Quentin Gralepois dit ALI
