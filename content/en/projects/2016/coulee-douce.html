water, dripper, black cotton sewing thread, variable dimensions


Coulée douce  is an almost invisible installation. A cotton thread stretches across the space; water droplets slide along it, their descent slowed before they crash to the ground and form a puddle, which grows and evaporates over the course of the exhibition. Ismaïl Bahri captures and directs the viewer’s attention toward a microcosm that takes some getting used to—a continuous movement, the formation of a living organism within the exhibition space.

An initial version was produced in Tunis in 2014, followed by a second version by the contemporary art center Les Églises in Chelles in 2014.
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
