David Horvitz

body: >-


néon, 10 x 350 cm


The piece presented at La Criée consists of letters attached in neon forming a sentence:   Whenever I take a shower I always wonder when the water was a cloud . The work highlights the wonder aroused by an element of everyday life that tends to become scarce, while questioning its source. Influenced by conceptual artists such as Bas Jan Ader or On Kawara, David Horvitz develops a nomadic and poetic work through books, performance, photography or internet. The images he presents are similar to postcards, they are exchanges between him and different audiences, memories of interventions he makes all around the world or a way to encourage us to think like conceptual artists. In the era of interactive digital technologies, David Horvitz invites us to understand our environment in a different way.

A first copy of this piece was produced in 2016 by the ChertLüdde gallery, Berlin. courtesy the artist and ChertLüdde, Berlin
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
