triptych video projected onto three screens HD video, 1:1.85 aspect ratio—3 mono sources, 1 hour 15 minutes


La Rhétorique des marées_Vol.2  presents the film, edited and projected as a triptych on large wooden panels. This installation gives it a sculptural presence, which is reflected in each shot; pebbles, seaweed, grasses, and mollusks fill the screens with their colors, textures, and volumes. The images, combined with sound—which is mixed as a medium in its own right—invite the viewer to physically experience the landscape, even to become one with its constituent parts. Through this artistic gesture, Ariane Michel “creates a sensory experience.”

Production : --Ariane Michel --La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
