granite, 30 x 30 x 30 cm


The sculpture’s form makes us question its reality. Is it a real stone or a fake one? The mystery may remain unsolved or be hinted at. The granite piece was actually carved from a hollow resin replica, lightening the overall weight without making it obvious. Joana Escoval’s forms are thus both evocative enough for our thoughts to latch onto them and unfold there with ease, and ethereal enough for our thoughts to then wander freely: open works* for wild thoughts**, so to speak
* with reference to Umberto Eco, L’œuvre ouverte, édition Le Seuil, collection Point, 1962 ** a term borrowed from Levis-Strauss, La pensée sauvage, édition Plon, 1962
Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
