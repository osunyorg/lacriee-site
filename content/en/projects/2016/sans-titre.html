Karolina Krasouli

body: >-


huile, gesso, graphite, feuille d’or sur toile, 280 x 420 cm


At La Criée, Karolina Krasouli is exhibiting a series of free-form paintings in which she uses the motif of the envelope, stripped of its original meaning. A sign that is both abstract and concrete—painted or applied with gold leaf on canvas—it appears as a solid expanse of color against monochromatic backgrounds that evoke medieval and Renaissance painting. Radically silent, radiating color as their sole form of perceptible communication, Karolina Krasouli’s paintings and sculptures step back from all language and display a vulnerability—a call to perception—sensitive to the slightest variation in light or breath.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
