body: >-


two videos on two screens, sound, color, looping, 58 sec


Each student embodied a chosen sculpture that was part of the set for Voyage stationnaire in a utopian setting. The students mimicked the position in space, the shape, and the color of their sculpture. They were filmed wearing white for the reverse side and clothing in colors as close as possible to the color of their chosen sculpture for the front side. The dual-screen video Incarnation , visible on two screens placed side by side, captures this choreography. François Feutrie

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes

Supports : -- ministère de la Culture / Drac Bretagne -- ville de Rennes / DEE Partnairs : -- académie de Rennes / DSDEN35 -- école Guyenne, Rennes
