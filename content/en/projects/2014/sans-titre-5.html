A2 poster (59.4 cm x 42 cm), matte paper, 135 g/m², 250 copies, plywood base and fir wood slats


A poster featuring all the drawings created by the students and the various objects photographed and collected during the exploration of Guyenne School. Some of these graphic elements were sculpted and created to my scale. They are part of the set design that makes up the final installation. François Feutrie
