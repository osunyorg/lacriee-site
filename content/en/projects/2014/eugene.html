mural


By turns a painter, graphic designer, illustrator, children’s book author, and creator of costumes and sets for the theater, his work reflects an interest in play and experimentation. At La Criée, he created his first mural, which depicts the motifs of the tree canopy through successive layers and blocks of color. Based on a fragment of a study of a tree named Eugène, painted from his window, the work plays with tones and motifs reminiscent of those of the Nabi painter and decorator Édouard Vuillard.

Production : La Criée centre d'art contemporain, Rennes
