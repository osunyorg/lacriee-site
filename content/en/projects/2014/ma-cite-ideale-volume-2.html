50 wooden cubes, each measuring 50 x 50 cm prints on transparent paper audio and video equipment interior design of the cubes using various materials 


Winner of the First Prize in the Audace competition for arts and cultural education for Ma Cité idéale, Lucas Grandin continues his exploration of the city, in collaboration with students from Rennes and Douala, Cameroon. As part of the next phase of his residency at the Jean Moulin School, Lucas Grandin conceived a work combining photography, video, and sound. He involved students and families from the Villejean neighborhood in Rennes and from Douala, Cameroon—where he was in residence in late 2013—in the creative process. Representations of the self, living spaces, and life journeys were at the heart of these experiments. This creative project revolves around language and the cube—a simple, sturdy architectural element that is stackable and uniform. The installation consists of cubes whose faces depict the heads, bodies, and legs of children from Rennes and Douala, allowing visitors to assemble or rearrange the photographs of the children’s bodies, which were taken by their parents in front of their homes. The children designed the interiors of each cube using a variety of materials. The installation is complemented by videos and interviews with residents of Villejean and Douala about their vision of the “Ideal City,” somewhere between reality and fiction.
