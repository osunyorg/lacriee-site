The Rennes Museum of Fine Arts is a French art and archaeology museum located in Rennes.
Its initial collection consisted of works seized during the revolutionary confiscations carried out in 1794 in the religious and civil buildings of the city of Rennes.
The current museum is housed in the former University Palace of Rennes, and since February 1, 2025, the museum has had an annex called the Maurepas Museum.
