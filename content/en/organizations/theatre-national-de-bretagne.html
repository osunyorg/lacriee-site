The Théâtre National de Bretagne (TNB) is a cultural institution in Rennes founded in 1990.
As a national theater center, its mission centers on creation, presentation, and training, and it operates at both the regional and national levels.
The TNB is a public venue featuring performance halls, a movie theater, a bar-restaurant, and an acting school.
